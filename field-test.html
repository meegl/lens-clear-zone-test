Clear field test

Click where it starts to look blurry, on each side. H: hide/show this bar. 1/2: switch eye.

How to take the test

  1. Put this page in full screen on the monitor you work with. With a tape measure, measure the width of the image (without the bezel) and the distance from your eyes to the centre, and enter both above. The grey bar at the bottom left should measure 10 cm; if it doesn't, correct the width.
  2. Sit in your usual posture with your nose pointing at the red cross. Your head does not move during the whole test.
  3. Choose OS and cover your right eye. Move only your eye to the left along a row of letters and click where they start to blur. Then do the same to the right.
  4. Switch to OD, cover your left eye and repeat.
  5. Also watch the gratings: if the vertical stripes blur before the horizontal ones (or the other way round), the image is being smeared in one direction.

Result


  

  
Degrees are calculated from the distance and width you enter, for a flat screen; on a curved one they are approximate. Millimetres on the lens are an estimate (about 27 mm between the eye's centre of rotation and the lens) to give the optician an idea; the real measurement is theirs.
