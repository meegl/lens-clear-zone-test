Lens clear zone test

Two tests you can do in your browser to find out how far you see sharply through each lens of your glasses, eye by eye, and explain it to your optician with numbers.

Clear field test

Full-screen chart. With one eye covered and your nose pointing at the cross, move only your eye and click where text starts to blur. Gives degrees and the % of the screen you see sharply.

Lens clear zone map

A drawing of a pair of glasses where you mark, on each lens, the band you see sharply and the areas that look blurry, smeared or doubled. Produces a summary and an image.

Open the map →

Getting reliable results

  1. Use the monitor you work with, at your usual distance. Measure the visible width of the screen and the distance from your eyes to its centre.
  2. Test each eye separately, covering the other one with your hand.
  3. In the field test, keep your head still; in the map, look at a fixed point and turn your head.
  4. Repeat each test a couple of times. If the results are similar, they are reliable.

Everything stays in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. These tests describe what you notice; the real measurement is your optician's.
